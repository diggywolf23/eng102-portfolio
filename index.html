Tiernon Wolf – ENG 102 Reflection Portfolio

Spring 2025 | Nevada State College @SECTA

Introduction

Hi, my name is Tiernon, and I’m currently a student preparing to study mechanical engineering at Texarkana after graduation. Throughout this semester in ENG 102, I’ve grown significantly as a writer—particularly in how I research and find credible sources to support my arguments.

One thing that helped this growth was our Sharing Knowledge Research Project—an assignment I’m especially proud of. As someone who builds cars and works on automotive projects in real life, I now approach writing the same way I approach a build: the right parts (evidence), a strong structure (organization), and a clean finish (clarity).

Critical Thinking & Reading

I faced challenges narrowing down my topic from the broad issue of social media and teens. Eventually, I focused on whether social media causes more mental health risks or builds stronger connections. This clarity helped guide my research and improved how I synthesized ideas in my Literature Review.

View Artifact: Literature Review

Information Literacy

My research process improved drastically this semester. I used credible sources like peer-reviewed journals, YouTube lectures, and TED Talks. I cited everything in APA format—which was a new challenge—and became more confident with integration and proper crediting of multimedia and academic work.

View Artifact: Annotated Bibliography

Writing Process

During my Literature Review draft, my instructor pointed out that my paper read like a list of sources. I revised by organizing by topic instead of article, which made the flow and synthesis much stronger. I learned that revision isn't just proofreading—it's rethinking the structure itself.

View Artifact: Literature Review

Authorship

For my Sharing Knowledge Project, I used images, charts, and videos to help visualize the research. I treated it like building a car: visuals were the bodywork and polish that enhanced the structure of the argument. This showed me the power of design and presentation in communication.

View Artifact: Sharing Knowledge Project

Rhetorical Knowledge

The most valuable takeaway from this course was learning how to adjust my writing for different audiences and purposes. In future engineering work, I’ll need to explain technical ideas clearly—this course has given me a great start in that area.

View Artifact: Research Proposal

Conclusion

ENG 102 helped me grow from a task-oriented writer into a purpose-driven communicator. I now approach writing like a builder—planning, sourcing, assembling, and fine-tuning. These skills will carry over into college, engineering, and any place I need to share ideas effectively.
